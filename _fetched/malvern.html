Our Prep School

Educating the Whole Child, Inspiring the Whole Future

Our inquiry-driven, bilingual, and holistic curriculum builds strong foundations while fostering resilience, creativity, and global mindedness through rich experiences in academics, wellbeing, and co-curricular life.

Full International Baccalaureate pathway offering the PYP, MYP and DP for a seamless educational journey.

Global Network

MALVERN COLLEGE HONG KONG

A Global Curriculum for Tomorrow’s Leaders

At Malvern College Hong Kong, our inquiry-based, transdisciplinary curriculum empowers pupils to explore ideas, ask meaningful questions, and make connections between local and global contexts. By integrating learning across subject areas and anchoring it in real-world relevance, pupils build a strong academic foundation, develop essential skills and self-management, and cultivate curiosity, character, and a lifelong love of learning while appreciating diverse perspectives.
