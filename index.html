    About Ryan Chi

    Ryan Chi, also known as Mr.RC, is a GTM hacker and artist who works between the United States, Dubai, Hong Kong and London. He cofounded insiders.bot, a prediction-markets aggregator for trading and copy-trading on Polymarket, and ran it as co-CEO until he exited in October 2026. In its first month of public beta it did $1.3M in trading volume without a dollar of marketing spend. Before that he was CMO and cofounder of Academic Labs, which raised $3.2M and hit a $350M peak valuation before he left. He is a founding member of City Protocol, where he now advises, and cofounded Showtime Watch and Sigmaboy.ai.

    Ryan studies Global Business and Computer Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) and went on exchange to the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania and to EDHEC Business School. He took MIT's AI course at 14, was Head Boy at Malvern College Hong Kong, and led 0xU, one of Hong Kong's largest university blockchain clubs. On X (@MrRyanChi) he writes about prediction markets, quantitative trading and Web3 strategy.
